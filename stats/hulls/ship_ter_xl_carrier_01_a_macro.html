The Tokyo-class carrier has been revised and refined since its first production run, most recently during the period of the Jump Gate shutdown, but at its core it remains fundamentally the same. The capacious front-facing hangar allows the carrier to quickly issue forth large waves of fighters to meet an enemy fleet head-on. In any fleet, a carrier is the central platform from which to coordinate large-scale fighter-based manoeuvres, and the Terran model does this with aplomb, whether the theatre of battle is on Sol's doorstep, or in a hostile system far removed from Terran domain.
