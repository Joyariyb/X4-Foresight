As the trend towards collecting natural resources with ships continued, the inefficiencies of the solution started to become clear. While a medium-sized ship had the advantage of moving quickly through mineral and gas regions, their cargo bays were simply not big enough for industrial-scale mining to be lucrative. Naturally, the Teladi stepped in to design L-sized resource collectors that could solve that problem, and the Magnetar is the Argon response to these ships; a standardised model that can be built in two variants, allowing it to support both asteroid mining and natural gas collection.
