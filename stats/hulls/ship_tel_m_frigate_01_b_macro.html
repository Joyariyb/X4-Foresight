The Teladi Osprey is a surprisingly effective frigate with significant cargo capacity. On this basis, it can be used as a well-armed and speedy alternative to a standard freighter for trade runs where prejudice is anticipated. The Sentinel variation compromises speed in favour of additional cargo space.
