The Xenon K is an unfortunately impressive destroyer with armaments sufficient to wipe out capital ships and stations alike. Its formidable shielding should be accounted for when devising counter-tactics. Notable Split warrior Ya t'Plp has earned the soubriquet "K-Dog" for her proficiency in engaging the Xenon K. Her widely published engagement records are required reading in military academies across the known galaxy. t'Plp's guidance on the K is "Destroy escorts first, then neutralise engines. Then attack, attack, attack. Must waste shields and hull before engines regenerate. Many guns will be needed."
