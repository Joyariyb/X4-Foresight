While largely superseded by more modern courier designs, the Argon Callisto has a strange, cult attraction. In no area does this ship excel, and yet thousands of owners gather periodically at Hatikvah's Choice to show off their various paint jobs and module modifications. The release of a Sentinel variant is seen by some as evidence that the manufacturer has found a means of monetising the Callisto's enthusiastic fan base, rather than an attempt to build a performance spacecraft.
