The Paranid Zeus is perhaps one of the fastest carriers available, making it an excellent choice as the keystone of any defensive or offensive fleet. The Zeus was designed specifically with this in mind, to allay growing Paranid concerns over the military growth of their many rivals. The Zeus is available in a Sentinel model, with a hardened superstructure for increased battle resilience.
