The Teladi Crane is a large miner with truly impressive load carrying capacity. Fulfilling a major part of Teladi primary resource acquisition, the Crane is available in models optimised for either mineral or gas collection. The Vanguard variant enhances the Crane's speed, which may benefit entrepreneurs who are mining resources far from where they are required.
