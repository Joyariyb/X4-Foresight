Developed as a near-perfect replica of Kyle Brennan's original ship, the Xperimental Shuttle is the definition of attention to detail, with every part - down to the smallest rivet - reproduced precisely. The project to recreate this iconic ship was undertaken by Kaori after using it in a simulation, which brought on intense feelings of nostalgia for Earth. Initially, this ship was fitted with an advanced teleportation device, derived from Sohnen technology, allowing the Xperimental Shuttle to traverse the universe with ease. After this drive was rendered unusable following an unfortunate accident, it was replaced with a standard-issue travel drive.
