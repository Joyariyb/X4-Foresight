When the Xenon destroyed a food production facility in Antigone Memorial, the sole survivor was protein engineer Tracie Harben. Having lost her entire family, and fuelled by vengeance, she dedicated herself to the creation of tools of destruction. Harben's most popular design is the Pulsar fighter, also known as the "Glass Cannon". A highly aggressive vessel, it prioritises armaments and speed above all else. No-one knew the Pulsar could be even more aggressively optimised until Harben surprised observers by releasing the Vanguard model.
