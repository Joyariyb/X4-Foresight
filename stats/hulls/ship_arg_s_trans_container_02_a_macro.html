While largely superseded by more modern courier designs, the Argon Callisto has a strange, cult attraction. In no area does this ship excel, and yet thousands of owners gather periodically at Hatikvah's Choice to show off their various paint jobs and module modifications. The release of a Vanguard model did not make the ship materially more useful, however, Callisto aficionados do seem to appreciate it.
