The Paranid Atlas is a large spaceship, specifically designed to repair and resupply fleets. This advanced vessel is equipped with cutting-edge robotics technology, and capacity for an ample supply of materials and resources. It can travel throughout space, effectively operating as a limited, but mobile, Equipment Dock. The Atlas Sentinel is a variant with a reinforced hull for increased durability and protection. The hardened structure allows the Atlas Sentinel to operate in more hazardous environments, and increase its longevity.
