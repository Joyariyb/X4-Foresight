The final ship shared with the Argon Federation by the Antigone Republic as part of their ongoing technology exchange, the new-look Colossus-class carrier was initially designed as such so that it didn't use much-needed resources that were sorely lacking in Antigone Memorial during the Jump Gate shutdown. With an array of medium and small docks and launch tubes, the Antigone Colossus is a significant improvement on other Colossus models, able to deploy ships to the battlefield at a much higher rate and collect and store them far more efficiently, at the cost of some of its previous firepower.
