The appearance of the Kha'ak can trigger a sinking feeling in the stomach or stomachs of any pilot, however accomplished. These "space bugs", as they are often described, attack in mystifyingly geometric vessels, and have, thus far, rebuffed all attempts at communication. This fighter has been dubbed the Protector and, like all Kha'ak ships, it is well-armed and trigger-happy.
