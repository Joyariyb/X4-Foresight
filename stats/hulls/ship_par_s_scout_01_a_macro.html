The Paranid Pegasus scout is extraordinarily fast, ensuring the "Peggy's" enduring popularity with explorers in particular. Unfortunately, being followed by such a speedy vessel risks inviting a rear end collision, an eventuality known by pilots as being "peggied". In response to demand from the most speed-addicted customers, the Pegasus was eventually released in a Vanguard model.
