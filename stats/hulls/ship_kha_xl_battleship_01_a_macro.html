This monstrosity has only been spotted a few times throughout history, before disappearing never to reappear again. It is unknown whether there is just a single Obliterator, or whether there are many more of them, lurking outside of currently explored space. The Obliterator is a hulking behemoth that is best compared to a battleship, although these comparisons break down when discussing the organic constructions of the Kha'ak. Very little is known about the Obliterator, as it is rare that anything survives an encounter with this ship. The fear that it causes is understandable, since on several occasions it has turned its devastating power on a planet, using its weapons to obliterate that planet entirely.
