Designed and produced by the Antigone Republic after the Jump Gates began to realign, the Veles-class freighter is more compact and self-sufficient than any freighter-class ship before it; the reason why it became part of the on-going technology exchange between the Republic and their Argon Federation allies. Built using a highly modular design, many ship designers have taken the base Veles model and reorganised it, as well as making their own changes to the internal systems, to sell an ever-growing number of variations on the ship. However, the Veles remains a popular choice among traders and station builders.
