The Ministry of Finance commissioned the Condor to be the mainstay of a carrier fleet. While the ship borrows heavily from Split technology, its implementation could not result in a less "Split" vessel. Heavily reinforced, the Condor is capable of launching a battle fleet under heavy fire. The Vanguard variant provides additional speed, allowing the Condor to get into, and out of, difficult situations with a little more ease.
