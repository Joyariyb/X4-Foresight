The Teladi Crane is a large miner with truly impressive load carrying capacity. Fulfilling a major part of Teladi primary resource acquisition, the Crane is available in models optimised for either mineral or gas collection. The E-model variation was introduced at a later date, and updated the superficial design to bring it up to par with Commonwealth standards that were established during the period of disconnection. The precursor version is slowly being phased out, but will remain available for interested third parties.
