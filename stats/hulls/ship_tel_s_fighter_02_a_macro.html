While the Teladi seem to have great affection for their Buzzard fighter, this is not without an awareness of its limitations. "Tough as brick, fast as brick" is the unofficial strapline adopted by Buzzard pilots, and it is often seen painted on hulls. The Buzzard seems to be designed to survive long enough for help to come rather than tackling assailants head-on. The modest additional speed provided by the Vanguard model is unlikely to make a difference to pilots' tactical options.
