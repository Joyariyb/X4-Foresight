Armed with the frighteningly accurate Kyon Emitter, the Kha'ak Queen's Guard most commonly attacks in swarm formation to devastating effect. The pseudo-insectile nature of the Kha'ak gave rise to the theory that they are directly or indirectly controlled by a Queen. This, in turn, inspired the name given to this vessel. However, the theory is as yet unverified.
